A few things worth keeping behind the till.

Practical paperwork to go with the course — the kind of thing a licensing officer or Trading Standards visit will actually ask to see.

Template

Refusal of Sales Log Sheet

A printable table for logging refused sales — date, time, description of the person refused, product, whether it was captured on CCTV, ID produced, who logged it, and the action taken. Covers alcohol, tobacco, solvents, fireworks, knives and other age-restricted products, not just alcohol.

Quick reference

Age By Year of Birth Calculator (2026–2031)

A till-side chart for working out someone's current age from their year of birth, for every year from 2026 to 2031. Birth years that land on 17–18 are flagged: check the exact date of birth before selling — if their birthday hasn't happened yet this year, they're still 17. Two copies per sheet, sized to cut in half and keep by the till.

Checklist

Due Diligence Checklist — Age-Restricted & Proxy Sales

A five-section audit for building and evidencing the "due diligence" defence: written policies & procedures, staff training & records, point-of-sale controls, proxy sales, and management oversight — with a sign-off, review date, and next-review field at the end. Built around the same due diligence concept covered in the course.

Refusal of Sales Log Sheet

A printable table for logging refused sales — date, time, description of the person refused, product, whether it was captured on CCTV, ID produced, who logged it, and the action taken. Covers alcohol, tobacco, solvents, fireworks, knives and other age-restricted products, not just alcohol.

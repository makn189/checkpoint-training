Frequently asked questions

The honest answers, before you start.

Is this an accredited qualification?

No. The Challenge 25 module is a plain-language training resource for all staff — it isn't accredited and doesn't hold personal licence status. It's not a substitute for the official Level 2 Award for Personal Licence Holders (APLH), which is a separate, accredited qualification.

How long does it take?

About 25 minutes for all 8 lessons, including the practice questions. Each lesson runs 3–5 minutes, so it's easy to fit between customers on a shift.

Do I get a certificate?

You'll see a completion screen once you finish all 8 lessons — a simple record that the training was completed. It isn't a formal certificate or legal accreditation.

Has the content been legally reviewed?

The course is grounded in the real Challenge 25 scheme and the Licensing Act 2003, but this build is mockup content and hasn't yet been checked by a licensing specialist. It should be reviewed before real staff rely on it for compliance purposes.

Is there more than one course?

Right now, just this one — Challenge 25 & Acceptable ID. A Licensing Annual Refresher and a Personal Licence Warm-Up are planned but not yet available.
